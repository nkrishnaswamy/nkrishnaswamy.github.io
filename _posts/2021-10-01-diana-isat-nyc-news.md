NYC's FOX 5 channel did a segment on AI in the classroom that focused in part on our Diana system and how we are expanding that expertise into situation-aware, interactive AI agents to help teachers in classrooms.  I'm not interviewed directly but the "CSU/Brandeis" work is shown in multiple clips (mostly taken from last year's AAAI demo video), and my advisor, James Pustejovsky, is interviewed extensively.  Unfortunately, the piece doesn't mention NSF iSAT directly, but this is where the work is being applied and currently funded from.  You can view the clip [here](https://youtu.be/w4Sa3GzOOgA).
