I got tenure. That's it. My mom put the letter from the University President on her fridge **under** my daughter's preschool graduation certificate.

![Tenure Letter](../../../assets/images/posts/tenure-letter.jpg?raw=true "Tenure Letter")

The process was long and tedious and involved putting together an exacting packet to specification and at least 10 letters: 5 from external evaluators and 5 from different CSU admins.

General feeling: somewhat anticlimactic, like *what do I do now?* But also of the ability to take greater risks and pursue things that are maybe a little bit crazy. And gratitude to live in a state that still has robust tenure and academic freedom protections.
