The DARPA FACT (Friction for Accountability in Conversational Ttransactions) program seeks to develop AI agents that model and mediate conversational dynamics in teams by revealing implicit assumptions and unspoken conflicts between dialogue partners.

Our team's approach is based upon modeling the common ground, or set of shared beliefs, that emerge within an team as they undertake a shared task. Our goal is to build an AI agent that can interpret multimodal signals to track the common ground, determine when a group has differing assumptions about the task, and insert naturalistic, relevant, and organic "friction" to prompt reflective reasoning and deliberation, in order to build epistemic cohesion within a team and enable better collaboration.
